OUR MISSION & STORY

BUILT BY RETAILERS, FOR RETAILERS.

StockFlow was created to eliminate spreadsheet chaos and bring enterprise-level inventory clarity to independent retail business owners worldwide.

OUR CORE VALUES

WHY SMALL RETAILERS TRUST STOCKFLOW.

We design every screen to solve real retail challenges faced on the store floor.

01. SPEED

Zero Operational Friction

Fast setups, intuitive barcode scanning, and simple stock balance adjustments designed for busy retail staff.

02. ACCURACY

Absolute Stock Accuracy

Real-time stock decrement across POS checkouts and ecommerce channels to eliminate out-of-stock surprises.

03. AUTOMATION

04. SUPPORT

Retail-First Support

OUR JOURNEY

FROM SPREADSHEETS TO AUTOMATED RETAIL OPERATING SYSTEM.

StockFlow was founded in 2024 by former retail store managers who were frustrated with over-complicated enterprise ERP software that required weeks of training and tens of thousands of dollars to configure.

We set out to build a streamlined, cloud-based platform specifically tailored for small boutiques, specialty shops, hardware stores, and multi-location retailers.
